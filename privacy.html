PLAIN-LANGUAGE PROJECT NOTES

Privacy and security

KinGuardian is a prototype. These notes describe what the current code does and its limits. They are not a promise of emergency response or a formal security certification.

Account information

The service stores your username, a password hash, recovery-code hashes, session hashes, and basic timestamps in Cloudflare D1. Passwords and recovery codes are not stored as readable text. Registration does not need an email address or phone number. Cloudflare Turnstile is required for sign-in and registration. Cloudflare processes browser and connection signals to help distinguish people from automated requests.

Trusted people and location

Trusted connections use usernames and require both people to accept. Each person chooses whether to receive SOS, Safe Journey, and location updates. Location is requested after you choose a location feature. Saved points are encrypted before they reach the database, but the KinGuardian server can decrypt them for a currently allowed recipient. This is not end-to-end encryption. Sharing stops at the chosen expiry or when it is stopped. Live updates pause when the page is hidden or closed.

Messages and notifications

KinGuardian can create in-app alerts for trusted people who allowed the relevant notice. Browser push works only when its keys, scheduled Worker, device support, and permission are set up. A push service accepting a message does not prove that a person saw it. KinGuardian does not automatically send SMS, email, or WhatsApp messages.

Emergency limits

SOS can save an alert and prepare notices for allowed trusted people, but this is best effort. KinGuardian does not call emergency services. Offline SOS stays on the device and cannot notify anyone until it reconnects and syncs. Do not rely on KinGuardian for urgent help. Call the emergency number for your current location.

Hosting, maps, and this device

Cloudflare hosts the website and database. The hosting operator controls the code delivered to your browser and processes service information needed to run the site. The interactive map loads map tiles from OpenFreeMap using OpenStreetMap data, so the map provider can see which map area your browser requests. Map tiles need internet and are not available offline. Saved offline snapshots are encrypted in this browser's storage, but they are not protected from malware or someone controlling this browser profile.

Missing protections

This prototype does not have email or phone verification, multi-factor authentication, an independent security review, or a verified global emergency-number directory. Recovery codes are the only account-recovery method. Keep your password and recovery codes private. If you lose all recovery codes and forget your password, the account may not be recoverable.
