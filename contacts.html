PRIVATE DATA VAULT

Unlocking your contacts

Your encryption passphrase is separate from your account password and is never sent to KinGuardian. The vault locks after 15 minutes without activity and when you leave this page.

If you forget this passphrase, this prototype has no recovery process and the encrypted contact data may be permanently unreadable. Store it safely before continuing.
