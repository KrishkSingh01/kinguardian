GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things to know.

Can anyone create an account?

Yes. Anyone can register as a member with a username and password. Public registration cannot create an owner account.

Does KinGuardian call emergency services?

No. KinGuardian is not an emergency service and does not call emergency responders. Contact local emergency services directly when urgent help is needed.

Will my contacts receive an alert automatically?

Not in the current preview. SOS events are recorded, but automatic contact alerts are not currently enabled. The site shows this limitation before you use the app.

How do I reset a forgotten password?

Use one of your saved one-time recovery codes on the password recovery page. KinGuardian does not currently send password-reset emails.
