SAFETY, WITH YOUR CHOICES AT THE CENTRE

Simple ways to stay connected.

Use the features that suit you. Sharing is controlled by you and your trusted members.

01

Make a trusted circle

Invite another KinGuardian member. The connection begins only after they accept.

02

Start a Safe Journey

Set a return time and choose trusted people who have allowed journey alerts. A missed timer is a reminder to check in with one another.

03

Choose location sharing

Start sharing only when you choose. Browser location is requested after your action and stops when you stop or the share expires.

04

Send an SOS alert

Cancel during a 10-second countdown. KinGuardian saves an in-app alert for selected trusted people; push needs setup and browser permission.

YOUR INFORMATION, YOUR RESPONSIBILITY

Built with privacy choices in mind.

Passwords are private, location sharing is opt-in, and this prototype does not sell your information. Keep your recovery codes somewhere safe. Location is encrypted in the database, but KinGuardian’s server can decrypt it for people you selected.
